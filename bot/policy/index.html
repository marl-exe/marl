Privacy Policy

Discord Bot

This Privacy Policy explains what information the bot may process when providing server automation features such as role management, voice-channel management, and bot commands.

Last updated: September 11, 2026

The short version

The bot processes only the Discord information needed to provide its features and keep the service reliable. It does not sell personal data, does not use advertising or behavioral tracking, and does not record or store voice audio.

1. Information the bot may process

Depending on which features are used in a server, the bot may process:

  • Discord user IDs
  • Discord server (guild) IDs
  • Role IDs and role configuration
  • Channel and category IDs
  • Voice-state information needed to create, manage, or remove temporary voice channels
  • Commands, interactions, and options intentionally submitted to the bot
  • Configuration settings selected by server administrators
  • Limited technical logs needed for reliability, troubleshooting, security, and abuse prevention

The bot does not need your Discord password and does not receive it.

2. Messages and commands

The bot is not intended to monitor or archive ordinary server conversations. Information submitted through bot commands or Discord interactions may be processed as necessary to perform the requested action.

If Discord provides message-related metadata required for a configured bot feature, that information is used only for that feature or for operational troubleshooting.

3. Voice channels and audio

The bot may observe Discord voice-state events such as a user joining, leaving, or moving between voice channels when needed for voice-channel automation.

The bot does not record, listen to, transcribe, or store voice audio.

4. How information is used

Information is processed only as reasonably necessary to:

  • Assign, remove, or manage roles
  • Create, manage, and remove configured voice channels
  • Respond to commands and interactions
  • Remember server-specific configuration
  • Detect errors, prevent abuse, and maintain service reliability

5. Data storage and retention

Server configuration may be retained while the bot is installed or while the configuration is needed to provide the requested features.

Temporary voice-channel state is retained only as needed to operate those channels. Operational logs, when created, are retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for troubleshooting, security, or service maintenance.

When the bot is removed from a server, associated configuration may be deleted during normal maintenance or upon a valid deletion request.

6. Data sharing and selling

The bot does not sell personal information and does not share Discord data with advertisers or data brokers.

Information may be processed by infrastructure providers strictly as needed to host and operate the bot. Discord itself also processes information according to Discord's own privacy policy and terms.

7. Security

Reasonable technical and operational measures are used to protect stored bot configuration and service data. No internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security, but access is limited to what is needed to operate and maintain the bot.

8. Your choices and deletion requests

Server administrators can stop further bot processing by disabling configured features or removing the bot from the server.

Requests to delete stored configuration or other data associated with a Discord user or server can be sent to the contact address below. Reasonable verification may be required before fulfilling a request.

9. Children's privacy

The bot is intended for use through Discord and is not directed specifically at children. Users must meet Discord's applicable age requirements and comply with Discord's Terms of Service.

10. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the bot's features or data practices change. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised when material changes are made.

11. Contact

The bot is developed and maintained by marl. Privacy questions and data deletion requests can be sent to dev@marlrivera.com.
