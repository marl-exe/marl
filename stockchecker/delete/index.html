Stock Checker Privacy Policy

Account Deletion

Delete your Stock Checker account

You can request deletion of your Stock Checker subscriber profile and the data associated with it at any time.

How to request deletion

Use the contact method listed in 14. Contact of the Stock Checker Privacy Policy and state that you want your Stock Checker account deleted.

Steps

  1. Open the Contact section of the Stock Checker Privacy Policy.
  2. Send a request with the subject Stock Checker account deletion.
  3. Include enough information to identify the Discord account used with Stock Checker, such as your Discord username or user ID.
  4. We may ask you to verify that the request belongs to that Discord account before deletion is completed.

Data that will be deleted

After your request is verified, the Stock Checker account data associated with your Discord identity will be removed, including:

  • Discord user ID
  • Discord username or display name
  • Discord avatar reference, when stored
  • Alert preferences
  • Subscription status
  • Active app sessions associated with the account

Data that may be retained

Stock Checker does not intentionally retain your subscriber profile after account deletion is completed.

Operational or security logs that are separate from the subscriber profile may remain temporarily as part of normal service operation. They are not used to recreate a deleted Stock Checker account.

No additional retention period applies to the deleted subscriber profile itself once deletion is completed.

Unsubscribing is different from deleting your account

Using Unsubscribe all alerts in the app disables stock notifications but does not delete your Stock Checker subscriber profile.

To remove the profile and associated stored account data, submit an account deletion request using the steps above.

Discord account

Deleting your Stock Checker account does not delete or modify your Discord account. It only removes data stored by Stock Checker.
