Receipt to Cloud Privacy Policy

Delete your account

You can permanently delete your Receipt to Cloud account from the app at Settings → Account → Delete account.

Account deletion cannot be undone.

What is deleted

  • Your Receipt to Cloud account.
  • Your receipt metadata stored by Receipt to Cloud.
  • Your active Receipt to Cloud app sessions.

What remains yours

Receipt images and CSV backups stored in your personal Google Drive are not automatically deleted.

Delete without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, continue to the secure Receipt to Cloud deletion service and verify with the same Google account used by Receipt to Cloud.

Continue to secure account deletion

Google verification is handled by the Receipt to Cloud backend so an email address alone cannot be used to delete another person's account.
